## Notes

* RCV ran with the CASP16 settings of `compare.py` (5 models, 40 trajectories, templates released before 2024-05-01). For R1255 and R1256 the library holds earlier cryo-EM structures of the same RNAs (PDB 8UYS, 8UYJ, 8UYM and 8QO5, released December 2023 to March 2024, 4.7–7.3 Å), which RCV used as templates (listed per target above); the CASP16 groups could use them too. Few valid trajectories (R1256) mostly mean failed loop closures on these coarse templates.
* Spread is only useful when the alternatives are real: on a rigid RNA, five different models mean four wrong ones. RCV builds its five models from different secondary-structure and template hypotheses on purpose; the CASP16 groups were asked for five ranked guesses of one structure.
