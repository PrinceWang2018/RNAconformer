## What this shows

* With templates, RCV's model 1 has a mean TM-score of 0.490 over the 15 targets: AlphaFold 3 0.480, trRosettaRNA 0.493; the expert groups Vfold (0.534) and GuangzhouRNA (0.526) are higher. RCV 0.7.2 (physics only) had 0.251.
* RCV is more than 0.05 ahead of AlphaFold 3 on 5 targets (R1211, R1221s3, R1224s3, R1263, R1264) and more than 0.05 behind on 6 (R1203, R1261, R1262, R1271, R1293, R1296); model 1 is higher on 7 of 15.
* Where RCV found a template (9 targets) its models follow the template closely, so accuracy is set by how similar the template is to the target: mean 0.641 (AlphaFold 3 0.599). On the other 6 targets, which the whole field found hard, RCV has 0.263 (AlphaFold 3 0.301).
* Without templates (physics, ViennaRNA 2D and junction geometry from the library) RCV 0.7.3 has 0.265.
* Speed: median 127 s per target (longest 433 s) for 40 trajectories on a 16-core laptop CPU (Intel Core Ultra 9 285H), no GPU; the structure library is 21.5 MB.
* RNAs solved in several conformations (R1203, R1255, R1256): [ENSEMBLES.md](ENSEMBLES.md). Size, hardware and features next to other predictors: [rcv_comparison.svg](rcv_comparison.svg) (`figure.py`).

## Fairness notes

* The CASP16 groups predicted blind in 2024. RCV was run in 2026; its template library and junction geometry were limited to structures released before 2024-05-01 (the start of the CASP16 RNA prediction season; all 15 reference structures were released later). The CASP16 groups could use the same structures.
* AlphaFold 3 (the AF3 server baseline, group 304) uses no RNA templates and was trained on PDB structures released up to 2021-09-30. Templates that RCV used and that were released after that: 7LJ3_B (2021-11-17), 7UO1_C (2022-09-28), 8DP3_R (2023-04-19). Vfold and GuangzhouRNA had all of them.
* Not fully blind for the method choices: the template rules (minimum coverage 45%, the rule for a second template covering an insert, ranking by agreement between templates) were set during 0.7.3 development with the CASP16 results visible. They are general rules, with no target-specific settings. The development benchmark (`benchmark/dev_templates.py`, 26 PDB chains, each with templates limited to structures released before it) is the independent check.
* RCV settings: 5 models, 40 trajectories ("thorough"), seed 42. Geometry parameters come from 178 PDB entries, none of them a CASP16 target (checked by release date and sequence). No MSA, no deep learning.
* TM-score above about 0.45 usually means the global fold is right; below 0.3 the fold is essentially random.
